Feedra Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2025

Feedra (“we,” “our,” or “us”) operates as a global news aggregator app that provides users with real-time news updates from trusted and publicly available sources around the world. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Feedra does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information to use the app. We may collect limited technical information automatically to improve performance and user experience, such as device type, operating system, app usage data, and approximate location (for regional news and weather).

2. How We Use Information

We use this information to display localized and language-specific news content, improve app performance, and diagnose issues.

3. Third-Party Content and Links

Feedra aggregates news headlines, thumbnails, and links from multiple news agencies and publicly available RSS feeds or web sources. When you click on a news item, you are redirected to the original publisher’s website. Feedra does not host or claim ownership of third-party content.

If you are a news publisher and wish to have your source modified or removed, please contact us at: feedranews@gmail.com

4. Advertising

Feedra may display ads from third-party networks. These partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show personalized ads. You can control ad personalization through your device or Google account settings.

5. Data Retention and Security

Feedra does not store personal user data. Analytics data is anonymized and used only to improve app quality.

6. Children’s Privacy

Feedra is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after updates means you accept the revised version.
