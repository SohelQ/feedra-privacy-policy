Feedra Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2025

Feedra (“we,” “our,” or “us”) operates as a global news aggregator app that provides users with real-time news updates from trusted and publicly available sources worldwide. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Feedra does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information. We may collect limited technical data automatically to improve performance and user experience, such as device type, operating system, app usage analytics, and approximate location (for regional news and weather).

2. How We Use Information

We use this information to display localized and language-specific news content, enhance app functionality, and diagnose technical issues.

3. Third-Party Content and Links

Feedra aggregates news headlines, thumbnails, and links from multiple news agencies and publicly available RSS feeds or web sources. When you click on a news item, you are redirected to the original publisher’s website. Feedra does not host, reproduce, or claim ownership of third-party content.

If you are a news publisher and wish to have your source modified or removed, please contact us at: feedranews@gmail.com

4. Advertising

Feedra may display ads from third-party ad networks. These partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver personalized advertising. You can manage ad personalization in your device or Google account settings.

5. Data Retention and Security

Feedra does not store personal user data. Any analytics data collected is anonymized and used solely to improve app quality and performance.

6. Internet Access

Feedra requires an active internet connection to function properly. The app retrieves real-time data from multiple sources to deliver the latest news and weather updates. Without internet access, some features may not be available.

7. Children’s Privacy

Feedra is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect or store any personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the app after any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised version.

9. Contact Us

Email: feedranews@gmail.com
